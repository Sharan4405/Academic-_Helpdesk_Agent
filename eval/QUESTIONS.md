## Rules, regulations and calendar (RAG)

**Q01. What is the attendance requirement to sit in the Major exam?**

- Reference answer: Under the July 2024 amendment, exam performance is no longer linked to attendance. A student is expected to have full attendance, but attendance must not fall below 75% in a course; below that, the instructor can recommend de-registration. Failing the 75% criterion no longer bars a student from the exam or forces a minimum C grade.
