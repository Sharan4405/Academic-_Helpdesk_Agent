Subject: Institute Guidelines for Campus Vehicle Possession and Driving Policy

In order to ensure safety, discipline, and smooth vehicle movement within the campus, Institute has

framed guidelines for vehicle possession, driving & parking. The same have been approved by the
competent authority and are hereby circulated for compliance.
1. Vehicle Policy for Students:

A. Students residing in hostels shall not own or use personal/borrowed motorized vehicles.
Exceptions may be granted for medical conditions or Persons with Disabilities (PwD), with prior
approval from DOSA on the recommendation of the Institute Medical Board. Access will be limited
to specified areas and monitored by the Office of Security and Office of Hostel Affairs. Such
students, if any, will follow the institute guidelines and security policy issued from time to time. If
permission is granted, all the rules for use of motorised vehicles in the campus must be followed.

If permitted vehicle is used by another student, the permission shall be withdrawn, penalty shall be
imposed and vehicle shall be confiscated. This would also call for expulsion from hostel for the
concerned student.

B. Unless for unavoidable circumstances, bicycles should be used for movement inside the campus.
No vehicles/bicycles shall exit campus after 9:30 PM, and such students must return to the campus
by 10:00 PM.

C. The day scholars should park their vehicles in designated parking areas/spaces. Moreover,
providing these vehicles to the other students/residents/any other person is strictly prohibited. The
tentative designated parking spaces for these are:

I. Near the Main Gate;
II. In front of the LHC-I and;
III. Opposite to the new faculty building (adjacent to the mathematics department).
2. For Project and Outsourced Staff:

A. Project or outsourced staff residing in hostels shall follow the same restrictions as applicable for the
students staying in hostels.

B. The project/outsourced staff commuting from outside campus should follow the guidelines
applicable for the day scholars.

C. Those staying in the staff quarters or guest houses may use personal vehicles, in line with Security
Office protocols and the guidelines applicable to the faculty /staff residing inside the campus.

D. Lending vehicles to students for use is strictly prohibited, and in case of violation, penalty measures
would be taken against such staff members.

3. General guidelines for motorised bike/vehicles

A. Vehicle owners must ensure their vehicles are roadworthy, insured, and have a valid RC.

B. Drivers must possess a valid driving license for the vehicle type.

C. Use of helmet is mandatory for both riders and pillion riders for two wheelers.

D. Vehicle owners must not hand over vehicles to students/staff residing in hostels.

E. All the institute stakeholders owning the vehicle(s) must obtain the vehicle(s) passe(s)/sticker(s)

from the Office of Security.

F. The visitors will be provided temporary passes for bringing the vehicles inside campus as per the

protocols of the Office of Security.

G. Commercial vehicles are prohibited on inner campus roads unless specific requests are made by the

units / department/ offices. These vehicles should use outer service roads.

H. The speed limit for the inner roads such as roads adjacent to the academic blocks, hostels, and

residential areas is 20 km/h.

I. For the outer roads such as road outside berms and adjacent to the campus boundary walls 30

km/h.

J. Speed will be monitored via cameras and speed check devices. Speed breakers will be placed at

strategic points.

K. For any violation of the speed limit, fines will be imposed as follows:

I. First time violation: Warning letter with copy to the concerning department.
II. Second time violation: Rs. 500.
Ill. Third time violation: Rs. 2000.
IV. Multiple time violations: recommendations for prohibiting the use of vehicle(s) by the
concerned driver in the campus.
4, Traffic Management and Parking

A. The Office of Security will provide information on designated parking spaces with due consultation

with the Office of Infrastructure Engineering. Any of such advisories should be followed in spirit.

B. The type B and type C residents should park their vehicles in available parking adjacent to the

housing blocks, specifically after office hours.

C. The Office of Security will issue color-coded vehicle stickers to regulate vehicle access to various

campus zones whenever required.

D. Suitable speed breakers will be installed as per the traffic movement and other requirements.

If any faculty, staff or project staff issues a vehicle pass for the vehicle to be used by a student, severe
disciplinary action would be taken against the person who is getting the vehicle pass issued. For issuance
of the vehicle pass, the copy of RC of the vehicle and the driving licenses of all possible users of the vehicle
should be submitted to Security Office.

All members of the Institute community are expected to strictly follow these rules in the interest of
safety, discipline, and the smooth functioning of campus transport.
