Subject: UG Specilization and Minors
The Competent Authority has accorded approval for thefollowing guildlines for
Speciliazation and Minors for the UG Students:

1. The option for pursuing a specialization or minor is available to the UG students at
the beginning of the 5th semester. Departments can offer 400 level courses as part
of Specialization Core/Electives for the 5th semester students. By exercising this
option, students can balance out the additional load of 10 credits for the
specialization/minor in their 5th, 6th, 7th and 8th semesters.

2. UG students also have the option to pursue a specialization or minor at the beginning
of any semester post 5th semester. In such case, any PE and OE courses completed
earlier, which are relevant to the specialization will be considered for the credit
requirements of the specialisation.

3. It is emphasised that specialization electives are not limited to lecture courses.
Students can take 6 credits worth of Project as part of specialization/minor. The
departments can offer the specialization/minor project as a single 6 credit project or
split it into two 3 credit project courses so that the students can balance out the
additional credit load for the specialization.

4. In case a student withdraws from a specialization/minor, the credits earned from the
specialization/minor courses would be considered in the Open Elective category for
the purpose of degree requirements.

5. Students also have the option to pursue the 20 credits of project work as part of the
department specialization of Engineering Innovation, at IIT Jodhpur, as well as in any
of its incubated startups and Section 8 companies. |
