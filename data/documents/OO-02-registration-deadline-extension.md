# Office Order -- Extension of Course Registration Deadline

Order No. AS/OO/2026-27/04, dated 30 July 2026.

## 1. Context

Owing to an AIS outage on 28 and 29 July 2026, a number of students were unable
to complete stage (i) of course registration within the regular window closing on
31 July 2026.

## 2. Order

1. The **regular course registration window for Semester I of 2026-27 is extended
   to 4 August 2026 (Tuesday), 17:00 hrs**, without late fee.
2. Correspondingly, the **late registration window closes on 10 August 2026**
   (instead of 7 August 2026). The late fee notified in OO-04 continues to apply
   to registrations completed in the late window.
3. Faculty Advisors are requested to complete stage (ii) approvals by
   **5 August 2026**, and the Academic Section will complete stage (iii)
   confirmations by **6 August 2026**.
4. The **add/drop deadline of 11 August 2026 is not changed**, nor is the first
   day of instruction of 3 August 2026.
5. The attendance rule in AR-02.2 is reiterated: attendance is counted from the
   first day of instruction, that is from **3 August 2026**, irrespective of the
   date of completion of registration under this extension. No attendance relief
   is granted on account of this extension.
6. Students who were unable to register due to pending dues are not covered by
   this order; such cases are to be settled with the Accounts Section first.

## 3. Supersession

This order modifies, to the extent stated above, the dates in the Academic
Calendar (SC-01) for Semester I of 2026-27. All other dates in SC-01 stand.

(Assistant Registrar, Academics)

Copy to: All Heads of Department, All Faculty Advisors, AIS Administrator,
Accounts Section.
