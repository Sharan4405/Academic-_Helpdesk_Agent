# Course Registration Procedure

## AR-06.1 Registration window

1. Course registration for Semester I of 2026-27 opened on **20 July 2026** and
   the regular window closed on **31 July 2026** (see SC-01 and Office Order
   OO-02 for the extension).
2. Registration is completed on AIS in three stages: (i) student selects courses,
   (ii) Faculty Advisor approves, (iii) Academic Section confirms. **A
   registration is valid only after stage (iii).**

## AR-06.2 Credit limits

3. A B.Tech. student may register for a **minimum of 15 and a maximum of 27
   credits** in a semester.
4. A student with CGPA below 6.00 is restricted to a maximum of 20 credits.
5. An M.Tech. student may register for a **minimum of 12 and a maximum of 24
   credits** in a semester.
6. A relaxation of the upper limit by up to 3 credits may be approved by the Dean,
   Academic Affairs on the recommendation of the Faculty Advisor, in the final
   year of the programme only.

## AR-06.3 Preconditions

7. A student may register only if institute dues for the previous semester are
   cleared and the hostel and library no-dues status on AIS is green.
8. Prerequisites listed in the course catalogue are enforced by AIS. A
   prerequisite waiver requires the written consent of the Course Instructor
   uploaded as an attachment before the close of the registration window.
9. A student cannot register for two courses whose **slots overlap**. Slot
   definitions are in SC-02. AIS blocks such a combination automatically.

## AR-06.4 Late registration

10. Late registration is permitted up to **seven calendar days** after the close
    of the regular window, with a late fee as notified in OO-04.
11. A student who has not registered by the end of the late window is treated as
    **not enrolled for the semester** and the case is referred to the Senate
    Undergraduate / Postgraduate Committee.

## AR-06.5 Audit registration

12. A student may audit at most one course per semester. An audited course carries
    an S or X grade, counts towards neither SGPA nor the credit requirement, and
    requires 75% attendance like any other course.
13. An audit cannot be converted to a credit registration after the add/drop
    window closes (see AR-07).
