# Office Order -- Credit Transfer for SWAYAM/NPTEL Courses and Semester Exchange

Order No. DoAA/OO/2026-27/03, dated 16 July 2026.

## 1. SWAYAM / NPTEL credit transfer

1. A B.Tech. student may transfer **a maximum of 6 credits** earned through
   approved SWAYAM or NPTEL courses, and these count only against the **Open
   Elective** requirement of CU-01.2 (see CU-04.5).
2. **Prior approval is mandatory.** The student files Form AC-16 before the
   SWAYAM registration window closes, with the course syllabus attached. The
   Department Undergraduate Committee certifies that the content does not overlap
   by more than 30% with any course already credited.
3. Only courses of **12 weeks or longer** carrying a proctored end-term
   examination are eligible. An 8-week course is not eligible for credit transfer.
4. The student must obtain at least **60% in the proctored examination**. The
   transferred course appears on the transcript as `TR` with the credit count, and
   **does not carry grade points**; it is therefore excluded from the CGPA.
5. A maximum of **one SWAYAM course per semester** may be taken for credit.
6. Retrospective credit transfer for a course completed without prior approval is
   not permitted under any circumstance.

## 2. Semester exchange credit transfer

7. Credits earned at an approved partner institution during a semester exchange
   are transferred up to a **maximum of 20 credits over the programme**
   (AR-11.9), mapped course-by-course by the Department Undergraduate Committee.
8. Exchange credits are recorded as `TR` without grade points and do not enter
   the CGPA.
9. The last date to apply for a semester exchange for Semester II of 2026-27 is
   **30 September 2026** (SC-01).
10. A student on academic probation (AR-08.4) is not eligible for exchange.

## 3. Combined ceiling

11. The combined total of SWAYAM and exchange transferred credits shall not
    exceed **20 credits** over the entire programme.

(Dean, Academic Affairs)
