# Course Catalogue Extract -- Autonomous Systems and Allied Courses

## CSL7620 -- Autonomous Systems

- **Credits**: 4 (L-T-P: 3-0-2)
- **Slot**: G (Tuesday and Thursday, 11:00--12:30)
- **Room**: LH-305
- **Instructor**: Dr. Rohit Bhagat, Department of Artificial Intelligence and
  Data Science (rohit.bhagat@iitj.ac.in)
- **Offered to**: M.Tech. AI and Data Science (Programme Core), M.Tech. CSE and
  Ph.D. (Programme Elective)
- **Prerequisites**: None formally; working knowledge of probability, linear
  algebra and Python is assumed.
- **Co-requisite**: CSP7620 Autonomous Systems Laboratory

**Syllabus.** Agent architectures and rationality. Deterministic and stochastic
search. Markov decision processes and value iteration. Partially observable
settings. Localisation, mapping and SLAM. Motion planning: RRT, RRT*, lattice
planners. Behaviour trees and finite-state controllers. Learning-based control
and imitation learning. Multi-agent coordination, task allocation and
communication protocols. Large-language-model-driven agents, tool use and
retrieval-augmented generation. Safety, verification and assurance cases for
autonomous systems.

**Evaluation.** Minor-1 20%, Minor-2 20%, Major 40%, course project 20%.
The course project is a team project of three to four students.

**Textbooks.** Russell and Norvig, *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach*
(4th ed.); Thrun, Burgard and Fox, *Probabilistic Robotics*; LaValle,
*Planning Algorithms*.
