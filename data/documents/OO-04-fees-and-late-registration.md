# Office Order -- Academic Fees, Late Registration Fee and Refund Schedule

Order No. ACC/OO/2026-27/02, dated 10 July 2026.

## 1. Semester fee due date

The semester fee for Semester I of 2026-27 is payable on or before
**25 July 2026**. Registration cannot be confirmed at stage (iii) of AR-06.2
while the fee is outstanding.

## 2. Late registration fee (AR-06.10)

| Days after close of the regular registration window | Late fee |
|-----------------------------------------------------|----------|
| 1 to 3 days                                         | INR 1,000 |
| 4 to 7 days                                         | INR 2,500 |
| Beyond 7 days                                       | Registration not permitted; case referred to the Senate Committee |

The late fee is waived for students covered by the AIS outage referred to in
Office Order OO-02, for registrations completed by 4 August 2026.

## 3. Other academic charges

| Item                                                   | Amount    |
|--------------------------------------------------------|-----------|
| Rechecking request per course (AR-10.2)                | INR 200 (refundable if the grade changes) |
| Duplicate grade card                                   | INR 300   |
| Transcript, per copy                                   | INR 500   |
| Bonafide / course completion certificate               | No charge |
| Migration certificate                                  | INR 500   |
| Replacement of Institute Identity Card                 | INR 400   |

## 4. Refund on withdrawal

| Time of withdrawal from the programme            | Refund of tuition fee |
|--------------------------------------------------|-----------------------|
| Before the first day of instruction              | 100% less INR 1,000 processing |
| Within 15 days of the first day of instruction   | 50%                   |
| After 15 days of the first day of instruction    | Nil                   |

No refund is made for a course dropped after the add/drop window or for a
withdrawn course (AR-07.10).

## 5. Mode of payment

Payment is to be made only through the payment gateway linked from AIS. Cash and
cheque payments are not accepted. Transaction reference numbers must be retained
for one year.

(Deputy Registrar, Accounts)
