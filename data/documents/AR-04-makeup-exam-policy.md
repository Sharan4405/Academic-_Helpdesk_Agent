# Makeup Examination Policy

## AR-04.1 Scope

1. A Makeup examination is a **replacement** examination for a student who missed
   a scheduled Minor or Major examination for a reason admissible under Clause 3.
   It is not an improvement examination, and a student who has appeared in an
   examination cannot sit the Makeup for the same examination.

## AR-04.2 Admissible grounds

2. A Makeup examination may be granted only on the following grounds:
   - **Medical**: illness, injury or hospitalisation certified by the Institute
     Medical Centre, or by an external registered medical practitioner with the
     certificate countersigned by the Institute Medical Officer.
   - **Bereavement**: death of a parent, sibling, spouse or legal guardian.
   - **Institute duty**: representing the Institute in an event approved in
     writing in advance by the Dean, Student Affairs.
   - **Examination clash** not resolvable under AR-03.4.
3. Travel, personal engagements, family functions, placement interviews,
   internship commitments and poor preparation are **not** admissible grounds.

## AR-04.3 Application procedure

4. The student shall:
   a. inform the **Course Instructor by email on or before the date of the
      examination**, where circumstances permit, and in all cases within
      **three working days** of the missed examination;
   b. submit Form AC-07 (Application for Makeup Examination) on AIS with the
      supporting certificate attached;
   c. obtain the Course Instructor's recommendation on the form.
5. The application is then placed before the **Makeup Examination Committee**
   (constituted under Office Order OO-03), which decides within five working days.
6. Applications received after the three-working-day window are rejected unless
   the student was hospitalised for the entire period, in which case the
   discharge summary must accompany the application.

## AR-04.4 Attendance precondition

7. **A Makeup examination is granted only if the student's attendance in the
   course is at least 75%** (or has been condoned under AR-02.4). A student
   debarred for attendance shortfall is not eligible for a Makeup examination.

## AR-04.5 Conduct and grading

8. Makeup examinations for Minor examinations are held in the remedial slot
   (Slot S) within two weeks of the original examination.
9. The Makeup Major examination for Semester I of 2026-27 is scheduled on
   **3 December 2026** (see SC-03). Only one Makeup Major examination window is
   held per semester; a student who misses it is awarded an F grade.
10. There is **no grade penalty** on a Makeup examination. The paper is set at the
    same difficulty level and the marks replace the missed component in full.
11. A student who misses the Makeup examination itself has exhausted the remedy
    and the pending I grade converts to F as per AR-01.9.
