# Leave of Absence, Semester Break and Internship Credit

## AR-11.1 Leave of absence

1. A student may apply for a **Leave of Absence (LoA)** of one or two semesters
   on medical, family or entrepreneurial grounds, using Form AC-20.
2. LoA is sanctioned by the Dean, Academic Affairs. The period of LoA counts
   towards the **maximum programme duration** in AR-08.7.
3. A student on LoA is not registered for any course, is not entitled to hostel
   accommodation, and does not pay the semester tuition fee.
4. LoA cannot be applied for retrospectively after the Major examination window
   of the semester concerned has begun.

## AR-11.2 Internship in the final semester

5. A B.Tech. student in the final semester with CGPA >= 6.50 and no outstanding F
   grade may undertake a **full-semester industry internship** in place of
   coursework, subject to approval by the Department Undergraduate Committee.
6. The internship carries **12 credits** and is graded on the basis of an industry
   mentor evaluation (40%), an Institute supervisor evaluation (30%) and a final
   presentation (30%).
7. A student on internship must still satisfy the total credit and category-wise
   credit requirements of CU-01. The internship credits are counted against
   Programme Elective credits, up to a maximum of 12.
8. Internship is not a ground for relaxation of the attendance requirement in any
   other course registered in the same semester.

## AR-11.3 Semester exchange

9. Credits earned at a partner institution during an approved semester exchange
   are transferred as per Office Order OO-05, up to a maximum of **20 credits**
   over the programme, and are recorded as **credit transferred without grade
   points** -- they do not enter the CGPA.

## AR-11.4 Return from leave

10. A student returning from LoA must complete re-registration at least **two
    weeks before** the start of instruction of the semester of return, and is
    bound by the curriculum version in force at the time of return.
