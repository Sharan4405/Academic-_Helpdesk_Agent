# Academic Performance Review, Probation and Programme Duration

## AR-08.1 Satisfactory progress

1. A B.Tech. student is in satisfactory standing if CGPA >= 5.00 and the student
   has earned at least **75% of the credits registered** up to that point.
2. An M.Tech. student is in satisfactory standing if CGPA >= 6.00 with no F grade
   outstanding for more than one semester.

## AR-08.2 Academic probation

3. A B.Tech. student whose CGPA falls below 5.00 at the end of a semester is
   placed on **academic probation** for the following semester.
4. A student on probation:
   - may register for at most **18 credits**;
   - must meet the Faculty Advisor at least once a month;
   - is not eligible for Minor / Honours registration (see CU-05);
   - is not eligible for semester exchange or internship substitution.
5. Probation is lifted when CGPA returns to 5.00 or above.
6. A student who remains on probation for **three consecutive semesters** is
   referred to the Senate Undergraduate Committee, which may recommend
   termination of the programme.

## AR-08.3 Maximum duration of the programme

7. The maximum permissible duration, including any Leave of Absence, is:

   | Programme                      | Normal duration | Maximum duration |
   |--------------------------------|-----------------|------------------|
   | B.Tech.                        | 4 years         | 7 years          |
   | B.Tech.-M.Tech. (Dual Degree)  | 5 years         | 8 years          |
   | M.Tech.                        | 2 years         | 4 years          |
   | M.Sc.                          | 2 years         | 4 years          |
   | Ph.D. (full time)              | 4 years         | 7 years          |

8. A student who does not complete the degree within the maximum duration stands
   automatically deregistered.

## AR-08.4 Repeating a course

9. A course in which an F grade was obtained must be repeated when next offered,
   or substituted by another course of the same category if the curriculum
   permits substitution (see CU-01.8).
10. A maximum of **two repeat attempts** is allowed in any single course.
11. Under AR-01.7, the better of the grades counts in CGPA, but each attempt
    consumes a registration slot against the credit limits of AR-06.2.
