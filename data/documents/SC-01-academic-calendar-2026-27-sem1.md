## Notes

1. Instruction is held Monday to Friday. Saturday is used only for the remedial
   and makeup slot (Slot S) and for compensatory classes notified in advance.
2. The total number of instructional weeks in this semester is **15**, which is
   the basis for the 75% attendance computation in AR-02.
3. Compensatory classes for institute holidays falling on a working day are
   notified by the Academic Section at least three working days in advance.
4. Dates in this calendar supersede any dates circulated earlier by departments.
