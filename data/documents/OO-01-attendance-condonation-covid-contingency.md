# Office Order -- Attendance Condonation for Institute-Nominated Events

Order No. DoAA/OO/2026-27/11, dated 28 August 2026.

## 1. Context

With reference to Clause AR-02.4 of the Attendance Policy, several departments
have sought clarity on absences arising from Institute-nominated participation in
the Inter-IIT Sports Meet (12--18 October 2026) and the Inter-IIT Tech Meet
(2--6 November 2026).

## 2. Order

1. Students whose names appear on the official Institute contingent list issued
   by the Office of the Dean, Student Affairs shall have the days of travel and
   participation treated as **condonable absence** under AR-02.4(iii).
2. The condonation applies **only to the dates on the contingent list**. Absences
   on other dates are governed by the normal rules.
3. The ceiling of **two condonations per student per semester** in AR-02.6 shall
   **not** apply to absences covered by this order. All other provisions of AR-02
   remain unchanged.
4. **The 75% attendance requirement itself is not waived.** Condoned days are
   excluded from the denominator of the attendance computation rather than being
   counted as attended.
5. Course Instructors shall record such absences in AIS with the reason code
   `INST-DUTY` so that the eligibility list of 13 November 2026 reflects the
   adjustment correctly.
6. A student who misses a Minor examination on account of participation covered by
   this order is eligible for a Makeup examination under AR-04.2(iii), and must
   still file Form AC-07 within three working days of returning to campus.

## 3. Clarification on the floor of 65%

The floor of 65% in AR-02.5 continues to apply to absences **not** covered by
this order. This order does not create any exception to the non-condonable nature
of attendance below 65%.
