# Conduct of Examinations and Examination Hall Rules

## AR-03.1 Examination structure

1. Every theory course has two Minor examinations (90 minutes each) and one Major
   examination (180 minutes). Minor examinations are held in a common Institute
   window; the Major examination is held in the end-semester window notified in
   the Academic Calendar (see SC-01).

## AR-03.2 Eligibility to appear

2. A student is eligible to appear in an examination only if **all** of the
   following hold:
   - the course is shown as registered and approved on AIS;
   - attendance in the course is at least 75% or has been condoned under AR-02.4;
   - there are no outstanding institute dues as on the date of the examination;
   - the student carries a valid Institute Identity Card.
3. The Academic Section publishes the **eligibility list** on AIS five working
   days before the Major examination window opens. A student not on the list must
   contact the Academic Section immediately; disputes raised during the
   examination itself will not be resolved in the hall.

## AR-03.3 Examination hall rules

4. Candidates must be seated **fifteen minutes** before the scheduled start time.
   Entry is barred after **thirty minutes** from the start time.
5. No candidate may leave the hall during the first **sixty minutes** or the last
   **fifteen minutes** of the examination.
6. Only the stationery, non-programmable calculator and material explicitly
   permitted on the question paper may be carried. Mobile phones, smart watches
   and any network-capable device are prohibited inside the hall irrespective of
   whether they are switched off.
7. Students are responsible for verifying that they are writing the correct
   question paper for the correct course code.

## AR-03.4 Clash of examinations

8. If two Major examinations of registered courses are scheduled in the same
   session, the student must report the clash to the Academic Section at least
   **three working days** before the first affected paper. The Academic Section
   will schedule one of the papers in the reserve session of the same window.
   Clashes reported later are handled through the Makeup examination route
   (AR-04) and no additional concession is granted.

## AR-03.5 Unfair means

9. Cases of unfair means are referred to the Standing Committee on Academic
   Discipline. Penalties range from cancellation of the paper to cancellation of
   all courses in the semester and suspension for up to two semesters. Procedure
   and evidence standards are described in AR-09.

## AR-03.6 Answer script retention

10. Evaluated Minor answer scripts are returned to students. Major answer scripts
    are retained by the Course Instructor for **one semester** after grade
    submission and are then destroyed. Any request under AR-10 (rechecking) must
    therefore be made within that period.
