# Add, Drop and Course Withdrawal Rules

## AR-07.1 Add / drop window

1. Courses may be added or dropped within **the first ten working days** of the
   semester. For Semester I of 2026-27 the add/drop window closed on
   **11 August 2026**.
2. An added course requires the approval of the Faculty Advisor and the Course
   Instructor. A dropped course requires only the Faculty Advisor's approval.
3. Attendance for an added course is counted from the **first day of instruction**,
   not from the date of addition (AR-02.2). Students adding a course late are
   cautioned that the attendance deficit is not condoned on this ground.
4. A course dropped within the add/drop window does not appear on the transcript.

## AR-07.2 Withdrawal from a course

5. After the add/drop window a student may **withdraw** from at most one course
   per semester, up to **two weeks after the Minor-2 examination**. For Semester I
   of 2026-27 the withdrawal deadline is **30 October 2026**.
6. A withdrawn course is recorded with a **W grade** on the transcript. It is
   excluded from SGPA and CGPA but the registration attempt is visible.
7. Withdrawal is not permitted from:
   - an Institute Core course;
   - a Programme Core course;
   - a course in which an unfair-means case is pending;
   - a course if doing so takes the student below the 15-credit (B.Tech.) or
     12-credit (M.Tech.) minimum load.
8. A withdrawn core course must be registered again in a subsequent semester in
   which it is offered.

## AR-07.3 Semester withdrawal

9. Withdrawal from the entire semester requires the approval of the Dean, Academic
   Affairs and is governed by AR-11 (Leave of Absence).

## AR-07.4 Effect on fees

10. No fee refund is made for a course dropped after the close of the add/drop
    window or for a withdrawn course.
