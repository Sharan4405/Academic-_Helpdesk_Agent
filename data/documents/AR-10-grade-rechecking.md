# Grade Display, Rechecking and Grievance Redressal

## AR-10.1 Display of marks

1. Marks for every component must be displayed to the class within **ten working
   days** of the assessment, and in any case before the next assessment of the
   same course.
2. Before uploading final grades, the Course Instructor shall display the total
   marks and the proposed grade cut-offs for at least **two working days** so
   that students may point out totalling or data-entry errors.

## AR-10.2 Rechecking

3. Rechecking is limited to verifying that (a) every answer has been evaluated,
   (b) marks have been correctly totalled, and (c) the marks have been correctly
   entered. **Re-evaluation of the quality of an answer is not permitted.**
4. A rechecking request is filed on AIS using Form AC-11 within **seven working
   days** of grade publication, with a fee of INR 200 per course, refundable if
   the request results in a change of grade.
5. The Course Instructor disposes of the request within ten working days. Where a
   change is warranted, a revised grade is submitted to the Academic Section with
   written justification.

## AR-10.3 Grievance beyond rechecking

6. If the student is not satisfied, a grievance may be submitted to the **Head of
   the Department offering the course** within seven working days of the
   rechecking outcome. The Head may constitute a two-member committee to examine
   the script.
7. The final appellate authority on evaluation matters is the Dean, Academic
   Affairs. Decisions of the Dean on evaluation matters are final.

## AR-10.4 Time limits

8. No grade change request of any kind is entertained after **one semester** from
   the date of grade publication, since answer scripts are destroyed thereafter
   (AR-03.10).

## AR-10.5 Grade change by the instructor

9. A Course Instructor may initiate a grade correction on their own motion within
   the same window, with the approval of the Head of Department and the Dean,
   Academic Affairs.
