# Open Elective Policy

## CU-04.1 Purpose and requirement

1. Open Electives broaden a student's exposure outside the parent discipline.
2. **The Open Elective requirement for the B.Tech. degree is 12 credits**, as
   stated in CU-01.2. This is normally met by four 3-credit courses.

## CU-04.2 What counts as an Open Elective

3. A course counts as an Open Elective for a student only if **all** of the
   following hold:
   - the course is marked `Open Elective` in the course offering list for that
     semester;
   - the course is **not offered by the student's own parent department**;
   - the course is not a listed Programme Core or Programme Elective of the
     student's own programme;
   - the course is at **300 level or above**.
4. A course offered by the student's own department may be taken for interest but
   is counted as a Programme Elective or as extra credit, never as an Open
   Elective.
5. HSS courses taken to satisfy the 9 HSS credits of CU-01.2 cannot be
   double-counted as Open Electives (CU-01.10). An HSS course taken **beyond** the
   9 HSS credits may be counted as an Open Elective if it meets Clause 3.

## CU-04.3 Timing and load

6. Open Electives may be registered from the **fifth semester** onwards. A student
   may register at most **two Open Electives in a semester**.
7. Open Electives are governed by the same attendance (AR-02) and evaluation
   (AR-01) rules as any other credit course.

## CU-04.4 Seat allocation

8. Where demand exceeds the declared seat capacity, seats are allocated in the
   following order of priority: (i) students for whom the course is a Programme
   Core, (ii) students in their final year, (iii) higher CGPA.
9. The seat allocation result is published on AIS before the add/drop window
   closes, so that a student who does not get a seat can choose an alternative
   within the window.

## CU-04.5 MOOC-based Open Electives

10. Up to **6 of the 12 Open Elective credits** may be earned through approved
    SWAYAM / NPTEL courses, subject to the conditions in Office Order OO-05.
    Prior approval is mandatory; retrospective credit transfer is not permitted.
