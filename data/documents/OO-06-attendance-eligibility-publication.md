# Office Order -- Publication of Attendance Eligibility List and Debarment Procedure

Order No. AS/OO/2026-27/12, dated 20 October 2026.

## 1. Order

1. Course Instructors shall freeze attendance on AIS by **13 November 2026**, the
   last day of instruction for Semester I of 2026-27.
2. The **attendance eligibility list** will be published on AIS on
   **13 November 2026**. The list shows, for every registered course, the
   student's attendance percentage and one of three statuses:
   - `ELIGIBLE` -- attendance at or above 75%;
   - `CONDONATION-REQUIRED` -- attendance between 65% and 75%;
   - `DEBARRED` -- attendance below 65%, non-condonable under AR-02.5.
3. A student shown as `CONDONATION-REQUIRED` must submit the request with
   supporting documents through AIS by **16 November 2026**. Decisions of the
   Dean, Academic Affairs will be published on **18 November 2026**, before the
   Major examination window opens on 20 November 2026.
4. A student shown as `DEBARRED` **shall not be permitted to appear in the Major
   examination of that course** and will be awarded an F grade (AR-02.3). The
   student remains registered for and must attend all other courses.
5. Debarment in one course does not affect eligibility in other courses.
6. No attendance correction request will be accepted after 13 November 2026, in
   line with AR-02.9.

## 2. Responsibility of invigilators

7. Invigilators shall verify candidate names against the eligibility list before
   issuing answer scripts. A debarred candidate found in the hall will have the
   script cancelled and the matter reported to the Academic Section.

## 3. Advisory to students

8. Students are advised to check their attendance on AIS **weekly** rather than
   waiting for the eligibility list, since a shortfall discovered on
   13 November 2026 cannot be remedied.

(Assistant Registrar, Academics)
