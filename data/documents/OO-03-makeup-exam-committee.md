# Office Order -- Constitution of the Makeup Examination Committee

Order No. DoAA/OO/2026-27/07, dated 5 August 2026.

## 1. Order

In pursuance of Clause AR-04.5 of the Makeup Examination Policy, the **Makeup
Examination Committee** for the Academic Year 2026-27 is constituted as follows:

| Role                | Member                                              |
|---------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Chairperson         | Prof. Debashis Nandi, Computer Science and Engineering (d.nandi@iitj.ac.in) |
| Member              | Prof. Rekha Iyer, Mathematics (rekha.iyer@iitj.ac.in) |
| Member              | Dr. Aparna Deshmukh, Mechanical Engineering (aparna.deshmukh@iitj.ac.in) |
| Member (medical)    | Institute Medical Officer, Institute Medical Centre (medical.officer@iitj.ac.in) |
| Member Secretary    | Assistant Registrar, Academics (academic.section@iitj.ac.in) |

## 2. Terms of reference

1. To examine applications on **Form AC-07** for Makeup examinations, along with
   the Course Instructor's recommendation and the supporting medical or other
   certificate.
2. To verify, in every case, that the applicant satisfies the **75% attendance
   precondition in AR-04.7**. Applications not meeting this precondition are to
   be rejected without reference to the merits of the medical ground.
3. To dispose of applications within **five working days** of receipt.
4. To authenticate external medical certificates through the Member (medical).
5. To publish the approved list on AIS at least **two working days** before the
   Makeup examination.

## 3. Procedure for students

Students are reminded that:

- intimation to the Course Instructor is required **within three working days**
  of the missed examination (AR-04.4);
- Form AC-07 is filed on AIS and routed automatically to the Course Instructor
  for recommendation, and then to the Member Secretary;
- the Committee does not entertain direct email applications from students. All
  applications must come through AIS.

## 4. Quorum

The Chairperson and any two members form the quorum. In cases involving a medical
ground, the opinion of the Member (medical) is mandatory and is recorded in
writing.

(Dean, Academic Affairs)
