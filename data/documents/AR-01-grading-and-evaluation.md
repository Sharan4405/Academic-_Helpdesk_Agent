# Grading System and Evaluation Scheme

## AR-01.1 Letter grades and grade points

| Grade | Grade point | Description                   |
|-------|-------------|-------------------------------|
| AA    | 10          | Outstanding                   |
| AB    | 9           | Excellent                     |
| BB    | 8           | Very good                     |
| BC    | 7           | Good                          |
| CC    | 6           | Average                       |
| CD    | 5           | Below average                 |
| DD    | 4           | Pass (minimum passing grade)  |
| F     | 0           | Fail                          |
| I     | --          | Incomplete                    |
| W     | --          | Withdrawn                     |
| S / X | --          | Satisfactory / Unsatisfactory (non-credit and audit courses) |

1. **DD is the minimum passing grade** in a credit course. A course graded F must
   be repeated or substituted as permitted by the programme curriculum.

## AR-01.2 Standard evaluation weightage

2. Unless the Course Instructor notifies a different scheme in the first week of
   instruction, the default weightage for a theory course is:

   | Component            | Weightage |
   |----------------------|-----------|
   | Minor-1 examination  | 20%       |
   | Minor-2 examination  | 20%       |
   | Major examination    | 40%       |
   | Assignments / quizzes| 20%       |

3. For a laboratory course the default scheme is: continuous evaluation 60%,
   laboratory examination 25%, viva voce 15%.
4. Any deviation from Clause 2 must be declared in writing in the first week and
   **cannot be altered after the Minor-1 examination**.

## AR-01.3 CGPA and SGPA computation

5. SGPA = (sum of credit x grade point for courses registered in the semester) /
   (sum of credits registered in the semester).
6. CGPA is computed over all credit courses taken at the Institute. Courses
   graded W or audited (S/X) are excluded from both SGPA and CGPA.
7. When a failed course is repeated, **the better of the two grades is counted in
   CGPA**, but both attempts appear on the transcript.

## AR-01.4 The "I" (Incomplete) grade

8. An I grade is awarded only when a student has satisfied the attendance
   requirement (AR-02) but could not appear in the Major examination for an
   approved reason under AR-04.
9. An I grade must be converted within the window notified for the Makeup
   examination. **An unconverted I grade automatically becomes an F grade** at
   the end of that window.

## AR-01.5 Degree classification

10. A B.Tech. degree is awarded with **Distinction** at CGPA >= 8.50 and with
    **First Class** at CGPA >= 7.00, provided the student has no F grade
    outstanding and has completed the programme within the permitted duration.
