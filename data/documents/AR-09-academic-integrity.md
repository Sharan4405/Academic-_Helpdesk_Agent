# Academic Integrity and Plagiarism Policy

## AR-09.1 Definitions

1. **Plagiarism** is the presentation of another person's words, ideas, code,
   data, figures or results as one's own without appropriate attribution.
2. **Collusion** is unpermitted collaboration on work declared to be individual.
3. **Fabrication** is the invention or alteration of data, results or citations.
4. **Unfair means in an examination** includes possession of unauthorised
   material, communication with another candidate, impersonation, and possession
   of any network-capable device in the examination hall (AR-03.6).

## AR-09.2 Use of generative AI tools

5. Use of large language models and other generative AI tools is permitted only
   to the extent declared by the Course Instructor in the course handout.
6. Where permitted, the student must include an **AI-use declaration** stating the
   tool, the prompts or the nature of the assistance, and which parts of the
   submission were AI-assisted. An undeclared use is treated as plagiarism under
   Clause 1.
7. Submitting AI-generated text or code as original work in a graded assessment
   where such use was prohibited attracts the penalties in Clause 9.

## AR-09.3 Similarity thresholds for theses and reports

8. For M.Tech. and Ph.D. theses, the similarity index excluding quotations,
   bibliography and self-citations must be:
   - **below 10%** -- acceptable;
   - **10% to 40%** -- revision required within one month;
   - **above 40%** -- resubmission after one semester.

## AR-09.4 Penalties

9. On a first established offence the Committee may impose: a zero in the
   component, an F grade in the course, or an F grade in all courses of the
   semester, depending on severity. A second established offence attracts
   suspension for one or two semesters.
10. Every established case is recorded in the student's academic file. The record
    is not reflected on the transcript unless suspension was imposed.

## AR-09.5 Procedure

11. The Course Instructor reports the case with evidence to the Head of
    Department, who forwards it to the Committee within seven working days.
12. The student is given a written statement of the allegation and at least
    **five working days** to respond, and is heard in person before any penalty is
    imposed.
13. An appeal against the Committee's decision may be made to the Director within
    **fifteen days** of communication of the decision.
