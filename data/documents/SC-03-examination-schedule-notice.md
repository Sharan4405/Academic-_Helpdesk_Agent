## 2. Makeup Major examination

The **Makeup Major examination is scheduled on 3 December 2026**, forenoon
session (09:00--12:00), in Exam-Hall-A. Only students whose Form AC-07
applications have been approved by the Makeup Examination Committee (OO-03) will
be permitted. The approved list will be displayed on AIS on 1 December 2026.

## 3. Eligibility

Students are reminded that eligibility to appear in the Major examination
requires **at least 75% attendance** in the course (AR-02.1) and no outstanding
institute dues. The attendance eligibility list will be published on AIS on
**13 November 2026**, the last day of instruction. Any discrepancy must be taken
up with the Course Instructor on the same day.

## 4. Instructions to candidates

Candidates must be seated by 08:45 for the forenoon session and 13:45 for the
afternoon session. Entry is barred thirty minutes after the start of the paper.
Institute Identity Card is mandatory. Mobile phones and smart watches are not
permitted in the examination hall.
