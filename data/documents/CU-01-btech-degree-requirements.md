# B.Tech. Degree Requirements and Credit Structure

## CU-01.1 Total credit requirement

1. A student must earn a **minimum of 160 credits** to be awarded the B.Tech.
   degree.

## CU-01.2 Category-wise credit distribution

| Category                               | Credits required |
|----------------------------------------|------------------|
| Institute Core (IC)                    | 48               |
| Programme Core (PC)                    | 56               |
| Programme Elective (PE)                | 27               |
| **Open Elective (OE)**                 | **12**           |
| Humanities and Social Sciences (HSS)   | 9                |
| B.Tech. Project / Thesis               | 8                |
| **Total**                              | **160**          |

2. **Open Elective requirement: a B.Tech. student must earn 12 credits of Open
   Elective courses.** These are normally completed as four 3-credit courses in
   the fifth to eighth semesters. The detailed rules on what counts as an Open
   Elective are in CU-04.
3. The 9 HSS credits are separate from the Open Elective requirement and are
   satisfied by courses offered by the Department of Humanities and Social
   Sciences.

## CU-01.3 Other graduation conditions

4. CGPA at the time of award must be **at least 5.00**.
5. No F grade may remain outstanding in any course counted towards the degree.
6. The programme must be completed within the maximum duration in AR-08.7.
7. All Institute and hostel dues must be cleared and the no-dues certificate
   recorded on AIS.

## CU-01.4 Substitution and excess credits

8. A Programme Elective may be substituted by another Programme Elective of equal
   or higher credit with the approval of the Department Undergraduate Committee.
   An Institute Core or Programme Core course cannot be substituted.
9. Credits earned beyond 160 are recorded on the transcript as **extra credits**
   and are included in the CGPA computation.
10. A course can be counted towards **only one** category. A single course cannot
    simultaneously satisfy the Open Elective and the HSS requirement.

## CU-01.5 Minor and Honours

11. Credits earned for a Minor or for Honours are **over and above** the 160
    credits. See CU-05.
