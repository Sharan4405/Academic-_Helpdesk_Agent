# Approved Email Templates for Student Requests to Course Instructors

These templates standardise the information a Course Instructor needs. A student
may adapt the wording but must retain every field marked mandatory.

## TPL-01.1 Mandatory fields in any leave or makeup request

1. Subject line containing the **course code** and the nature of the request.
2. Student's **full name, roll number and programme**.
3. **Exact dates** of absence (from and to), not a vague duration.
4. The **reason**, stated plainly.
5. The **supporting document** that will be submitted, and when.
6. For a missed examination: an explicit statement that **Form AC-07 will be
   filed on AIS** (AR-04.4).
7. A copy to the Academic Section (academic.section@iitj.ac.in) and the Faculty
   Advisor (AR-05.5).

## TPL-01.2 Template A -- Sick leave intimation (short leave, no examination missed)

```
Subject: [<COURSE_CODE>] Sick leave request for <N> day(s): <FROM_DATE> to <TO_DATE>

Respected <INSTRUCTOR_NAME>,

I am <STUDENT_NAME> (Roll No. <ROLL_NO>, <PROGRAMME>), registered for
<COURSE_CODE> <COURSE_TITLE> in Semester I of 2026-27.

I am unwell and have been advised rest, and I will therefore be unable to attend
classes from <FROM_DATE> to <TO_DATE> (<N> days). I request you to kindly grant
me leave for this period.

I am consulting the Institute Medical Centre and will submit the medical
documentation within three working days of resuming classes, as required under
Clause AR-05.8 of the Leave Policy.

I am aware that approved leave does not by itself add to my attendance
(AR-05.11), and I will ensure that my attendance in the course stays above the
required 75%. I will also catch up on the material covered during my absence.

Thank you for your consideration.

Respectfully,
<STUDENT_NAME>
Roll No. <ROLL_NO>, <PROGRAMME>
<STUDENT_EMAIL>

Cc: academic.section@iitj.ac.in; Faculty Advisor
```

## TPL-01.3 Template B -- Makeup examination request (examination missed)

```
Subject: [<COURSE_CODE>] Request for Makeup <EXAM_TYPE> examination -- missed on <EXAM_DATE>

Respected <INSTRUCTOR_NAME>,

I am <STUDENT_NAME> (Roll No. <ROLL_NO>, <PROGRAMME>), registered for
<COURSE_CODE> <COURSE_TITLE>.

I was unable to appear in the <EXAM_TYPE> examination of this course held on
<EXAM_DATE> at <EXAM_TIME> because of <REASON>. <SUPPORTING_DOCUMENT_SENTENCE>

I request your recommendation on my application for a Makeup examination. I am
filing Form AC-07 on AIS today with the supporting certificate attached, within
the three-working-day window prescribed in Clause AR-04.4, so that it may be
placed before the Makeup Examination Committee.

My attendance in the course is above the 75% required under Clause AR-04.7.

Thank you for your consideration.

Respectfully,
<STUDENT_NAME>
Roll No. <ROLL_NO>, <PROGRAMME>
<STUDENT_EMAIL>

Cc: academic.section@iitj.ac.in; Faculty Advisor
```

## TPL-01.4 Template C -- Attendance condonation request

```
Subject: [<COURSE_CODE>] Request for attendance condonation under AR-02.4

Respected <INSTRUCTOR_NAME>,

I am <STUDENT_NAME> (Roll No. <ROLL_NO>, <PROGRAMME>). My attendance in
<COURSE_CODE> <COURSE_TITLE> currently stands at <ATTENDANCE_PERCENT>%, which is
below the 75% required to appear in the Major examination.

The shortfall arose from <REASON> during <FROM_DATE> to <TO_DATE>, for which the
supporting documents are attached. I request your recommendation on my
condonation request (Form AC-22) so that it may be placed before the Dean,
Academic Affairs, as provided in Clause AR-02.4.

Respectfully,
<STUDENT_NAME>
Roll No. <ROLL_NO>, <PROGRAMME>
<STUDENT_EMAIL>

Cc: academic.section@iitj.ac.in; Faculty Advisor
```

## TPL-01.5 What not to write

8. Do not ask for attendance to be "marked present" -- no rule permits this.
9. Do not ask for a change in the evaluation weightage; it is frozen after
   Minor-1 (AR-01.4).
10. Do not promise documents that cannot be produced. A request without the
    certificate required by AR-04.2 will be rejected by the Committee.
