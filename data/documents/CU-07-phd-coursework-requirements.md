# Ph.D. Coursework and Comprehensive Examination Requirements

## CU-07.1 Coursework

1. A Ph.D. scholar admitted with a master's degree must complete **12 credits**
   of coursework; a scholar admitted with a bachelor's degree must complete
   **24 credits**.
2. Coursework must be completed within the first **three semesters** of
   registration.
3. All coursework must be at **600 level or above**, with at most one 400- or
   500-level course permitted on the written recommendation of the Student
   Research Committee (SRC).
4. The minimum passing grade in Ph.D. coursework is **BC**, which is higher than
   the DD threshold applicable to other programmes (AR-01.1). A grade of CC or
   below requires the course to be repeated or substituted.
5. A CGPA of **7.00** in coursework is required before the comprehensive
   examination can be taken.

## CU-07.2 Comprehensive examination

6. The comprehensive examination is taken after coursework and within **four
   semesters** of registration. It has a written and an oral component.
7. A scholar failing the comprehensive examination may take it once more within
   six months. A second failure leads to termination of registration.

## CU-07.3 Attendance for scholars

8. Ph.D. scholars registered in a credit course are subject to the same 75%
   attendance requirement under AR-02. Research work is not a ground for
   exemption.

## CU-07.4 Synopsis and thesis

9. The synopsis seminar is held at least **three months** before thesis
   submission and requires SRC approval.
10. A minimum of **one journal publication or two peer-reviewed conference
    publications** from the thesis work is required before submission.

## CU-07.5 Annual progress review

11. Progress is reviewed by the SRC every six months. Two consecutive
    unsatisfactory reviews lead to a show-cause notice from the Dean, Academic
    Affairs.
