# Timetable Slot System -- Semester I, 2026-27

## SC-02.1 Principle

1. Every course is assigned a **slot**. All courses in the same slot meet at the
   same time, and therefore **two courses in the same slot cannot be registered
   together** (AR-06.9). AIS enforces this automatically.

## SC-02.5 Lunch break and reserved time

2. 12:30--14:00 on all instructional days is the lunch break. No lecture,
   tutorial or laboratory may be scheduled in this period.
3. Wednesday 17:00--18:30 is reserved for departmental seminars and no course may
   be scheduled in it.

## SC-02.6 Slot changes

4. A Course Instructor may not change the slot of a course after the first day of
   instruction. A one-off compensatory class in a different time must be notified
   to the class and the Timetable Unit at least three working days in advance and
   must not clash with any other course registered by the affected students.
