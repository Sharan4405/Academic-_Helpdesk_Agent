# B.Tech. Artificial Intelligence and Data Science -- Curriculum

## CU-03.1 Credit summary

Total 160 credits, distributed as in CU-01.2. The Open Elective requirement is
**12 credits** and the HSS requirement is **9 credits**, identical to all other
B.Tech. branches.

## CU-03.2 Programme Core courses

| Code    | Title                               | Credits | Typical semester |
|---------|-------------------------------------|---------|------------------|
| AIL2010 | Foundations of Machine Learning     | 4       | 3                |
| AIL2020 | Data Structures for Data Science    | 4       | 3                |
| AIL2030 | Statistical Inference               | 3       | 4                |
| AIL3010 | Deep Learning                       | 4       | 5                |
| AIL3020 | Data Engineering and Big Data       | 3       | 5                |
| AIL3030 | Optimisation for Machine Learning   | 3       | 6                |
| AIL3040 | Natural Language Processing         | 3       | 6                |

## CU-03.3 Programme Electives (indicative)

AIL4010 Generative AI and Foundation Models, AIL4030 Time Series Analysis,
AIL4040 Reinforcement Learning (UG), AIL4050 Computer Vision, AIL4060 Graph
Machine Learning. 27 Programme Elective credits are required.

## CU-03.4 Mandatory ethics component

A student of this programme must earn at least **3 credits from an approved AI
ethics or society course**. HSL4110 Ethics of Artificial Intelligence satisfies
this requirement and is counted against the HSS requirement of CU-01.2, not
against the Open Elective requirement.

## CU-03.5 Capstone

AIP4910 Capstone Project Part I (4 credits) and AIP4920 Capstone Project Part II
(4 credits). The capstone must involve a dataset or deployment component approved
by the Department Undergraduate Committee.

## CU-03.6 Bridge courses

A student admitted through the lateral or branch-change route without AIL2010
must complete it as a bridge course in the first available semester. Bridge
credits count as Programme Core.
