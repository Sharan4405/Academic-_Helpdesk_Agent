## 3. Open Electives floated across the Institute

CSL4030, AIL4020, EEL4020, MEL4020, MTL3010, PHL4010, BBL4010, CEL4010, HSL4010
and HSL4220. Students are reminded of CU-04.3: a course offered by the student's
own parent department does not count towards the Open Elective requirement.

## 4. Seat capacity

Unless otherwise notified, each elective has a capacity of 60 students, and each
laboratory section a capacity of 30. Allocation follows CU-04.4.
