## CU-02.3 Programme Electives (indicative)

CSL4010 Compiler Design, CSL4020 Information Security, CSL4040 Parallel and
Distributed Computing, CSL4050 Cloud Computing, CSL4060 Computer Graphics,
CSL4070 Human-Computer Interaction. A student must earn 27 Programme Elective
credits, of which **at least 12 must be at 400 level or above**.

## CU-02.4 Open Elective

A CSE student must earn 12 Open Elective credits from courses **outside the
Department of Computer Science and Engineering**. Consequently CSL4030 Software
Engineering, although marked Open Elective in the offering list, counts as a
Programme Elective for a CSE student (see CU-04.4).

## CU-02.5 B.Tech. Project

CSP4910 B.Tech. Project Part I (4 credits, semester 7) and CSP4920 B.Tech.
Project Part II (4 credits, semester 8). Part II may be registered only after a
DD grade or better in Part I.

## CU-02.6 Laboratory requirement

Every Programme Core course with a non-zero P component in its L-T-P structure
carries a mandatory laboratory requirement. A student failing the laboratory
component fails the course irrespective of the theory marks.
