# M.Tech. Degree Requirements

## CU-06.1 Total credit requirement

1. An M.Tech. student must earn a **minimum of 68 credits**:

| Category                     | Credits |
|------------------------------|---------|
| Programme Core               | 18      |
| Programme Elective           | 18      |
| Open / Interdisciplinary Elective | 6  |
| Seminar                      | 2       |
| M.Tech. Thesis               | 24      |
| **Total**                    | **68**  |

2. The Open / Interdisciplinary Elective requirement for M.Tech. is **6 credits**
   and is distinct from the 12-credit B.Tech. Open Elective requirement in
   CU-01.2. The two should not be confused.

## CU-06.3 Thesis

3. The thesis is registered over the third and fourth semesters as MTP7910
   (12 credits) and MTP7920 (12 credits).
4. A mid-term thesis review is held in the third semester. An unsatisfactory
   review requires the student to repeat the review after three months.
5. Thesis submission requires a similarity index below 10% (AR-09.8) and a
   recommendation from the supervisor.

## CU-06.4 Performance requirement

6. CGPA at the time of award must be **at least 6.00**.
7. A student with more than two F grades in the coursework phase is referred to
   the Senate Postgraduate Committee.

## CU-06.5 Teaching assistantship

8. A student receiving an Institute assistantship must discharge **eight hours
   per week** of teaching assistantship duty. Unsatisfactory TA performance,
   certified by the Course Instructor, may lead to withholding of the
   assistantship for one month.
