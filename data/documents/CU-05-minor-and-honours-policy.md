# Minor and Honours Policy

## CU-05.1 Minor

1. A **Minor** is a certified specialisation in a discipline other than the
   student's parent discipline, requiring **15 additional credits** earned from a
   declared Minor basket.
2. Of the 15 credits, at least **9 must be at 300 level or above** and at least
   one course must be a laboratory or project course where the basket offers one.
3. Eligibility: CGPA >= 7.00 at the end of the fourth semester, and not on
   academic probation (AR-08.2).
4. A student declares the Minor using Form AC-14 before the start of the fifth
   semester. A declared Minor may be withdrawn once, before the start of the
   seventh semester.
5. Minor credits are **over and above** the 160 credits of CU-01.1 and cannot be
   double-counted as Open Elective or HSS credits.

## CU-05.2 Honours

6. **Honours** in the parent discipline requires **18 additional credits** of
   advanced coursework and research, of which at least 6 credits must be a
   research project supervised by a faculty member of the parent department.
7. Eligibility: CGPA >= 8.00 at the end of the fourth semester, sustained until
   the award.
8. Honours is recorded on the degree certificate as *B.Tech. (Honours)*.

## CU-05.3 Common conditions

9. A student may pursue **either a Minor or Honours, not both**.
10. All Minor and Honours courses must be completed with a grade of DD or better.
    A course failed in the Minor basket may be substituted once.
11. If the additional credit requirement is not met by the end of the programme,
    the courses already completed are recorded as extra credits and no Minor or
    Honours is awarded.
12. The credit limit of AR-06.2 (maximum 27 credits per semester for B.Tech.) is
    not relaxed for Minor or Honours students.
