# Student Leave, Sick Leave and Medical Certification Policy

## AR-05.1 Categories of leave

1. **Sick leave (short)** -- up to three consecutive days of absence on medical
   grounds.
2. **Sick leave (long)** -- more than three consecutive days, or any absence
   involving hospitalisation.
3. **Casual / personal leave** -- up to two days per semester for unavoidable
   personal reasons.
4. **Leave of absence (semester break)** -- a full semester or more; governed by
   AR-11.

## AR-05.2 Who to inform

5. For any absence affecting class work, the student must inform **each affected
   Course Instructor by email**, with a copy to the Academic Section
   (academic.section@iitj.ac.in) and to the Faculty Advisor.
6. The email must state: student name, roll number, programme, course code(s)
   affected, the exact dates of absence, the reason, and what supporting document
   will follow. For hostel residents, the Hostel Warden is also to be informed.
7. Informing the Course Instructor is the student's own responsibility. A message
   passed through a classmate or a message on a class group is not a valid
   intimation.

## AR-05.3 Documentation

8. Sick leave (short) requires a prescription or consultation slip from the
   **Institute Medical Centre**, submitted within **three working days** of
   returning to class.
9. Sick leave (long) requires a medical certificate stating the period of
   unfitness, and a **fitness-to-resume certificate**. An external certificate
   must be countersigned by the Institute Medical Officer.
10. Casual leave requires prior written approval of the Faculty Advisor; it is
    not granted retrospectively.

## AR-05.4 Effect of leave

11. Approved leave regularises the absence for disciplinary purposes. It **does
    not create attendance credit** -- the student remains subject to the 75%
    requirement under AR-02, and may only seek condonation under AR-02.4.
12. Approved leave covering a scheduled examination is the basis for a Makeup
    examination application under AR-04. The leave intimation does **not** itself
    constitute a Makeup application; Form AC-07 must be filed separately.
13. Assignment and project deadlines falling within an approved sick leave period
    are extended by the number of days of leave, unless the Course Instructor has
    declared the assessment non-extendable in the course handout.

## AR-05.5 Record keeping

14. The Academic Section maintains the leave record on AIS. Students may view
    their own leave history under *AIS > My Profile > Leave Record*.
