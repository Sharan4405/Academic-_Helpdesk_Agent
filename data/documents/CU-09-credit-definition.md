# Definition of a Credit and Course Numbering Scheme

## CU-09.1 Credit definition

1. Credits are computed from the L-T-P structure of a course:
   - **L** (lecture): 1 credit per contact hour per week;
   - **T** (tutorial): 1 credit per contact hour per week;
   - **P** (practical / laboratory): 1 credit per **two** contact hours per week.
2. Thus a course with L-T-P = 3-0-2 carries 3 + 0 + 1 = **4 credits**, and a
   course with L-T-P = 0-0-4 carries **2 credits**.
3. One credit corresponds to a notional student workload of 25 to 30 hours per
   semester including self-study.

## CU-09.2 Course code structure

4. A course code has the form `XXYNNNN`:
   - the first two letters identify the offering department --
     `CS` Computer Science and Engineering, `AI` Artificial Intelligence and Data
     Science, `EE` Electrical Engineering, `ME` Mechanical Engineering,
     `MT` Mathematics, `PH` Physics, `CY` Chemistry, `BB` Bioscience and
     Bioengineering, `HS` Humanities and Social Sciences, `CE` Civil and
     Infrastructure Engineering;
   - the third letter is the course nature -- `L` lecture-based course,
     `P` practical / laboratory / project course, `S` seminar;
   - the four digits give the level and the serial number.

5. **Level is read from the first digit** of the numeric part:

| First digit | Level | Intended audience                     |
|-------------|-------|---------------------------------------|
| 1           | 100   | B.Tech. first year                    |
| 2           | 200   | B.Tech. second year                   |
| 3           | 300   | B.Tech. third year                    |
| 4           | 400   | B.Tech. fourth year / early PG        |
| 5           | 500   | Master's (M.A., M.Sc.)                |
| 6, 7        | 600, 700 | M.Tech. and Ph.D.                  |

6. Example: `HSL4110` is a **400-level** lecture course offered by the Department
   of Humanities and Social Sciences. `CSP7620` is a 700-level practical course
   offered by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

## CU-09.3 Non-credit courses

7. Courses carrying zero credits (induction, ethics orientation, professional
   practice) are graded S or X and are mandatory for award of the degree even
   though they carry no credit weight.
