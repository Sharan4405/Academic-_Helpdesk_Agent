# Attendance Policy for Course Work and Examinations

## AR-02.1 Minimum attendance requirement

1. A student must maintain a minimum of **75% attendance** in every registered
   course, computed separately for lecture, tutorial and laboratory components.
2. Attendance is counted from the first day of instruction as notified in the
   Academic Calendar, **irrespective of the date on which the student completed
   registration**.
3. **A student whose attendance in a course is below 75% on the last day of
   instruction shall not be permitted to appear in the Major examination of that
   course** and shall be awarded an **F grade** in that course.

## AR-02.2 Condonation of shortfall

4. Attendance between **65% and 75%** may be condoned by the Dean, Academic
   Affairs (DoAA) on the written recommendation of the Course Instructor, in the
   following cases only:
   - hospitalisation or documented illness, supported by a certificate issued or
     countersigned by the **Institute Medical Centre**;
   - death or critical illness of a parent, sibling or spouse;
   - participation in an Institute-nominated event (inter-IIT sports, approved
     conference presentation, national-level competition) with prior written
     approval.
5. Attendance **below 65% is not condonable under any circumstance**. No appeal
   against this clause shall be entertained.
6. Condonation is a remedy of exception, not a right. A maximum of **two
   condonations per student per semester** may be granted.

## AR-02.3 Attendance for laboratory courses

7. For laboratory and studio courses, a student must attend a minimum of 75% of
   the scheduled sessions **and** complete all mandatory experiments. A missed
   experiment must be made up in the remedial slot (Slot S) within two weeks.

## AR-02.4 Display and correction of attendance

8. Course Instructors shall upload attendance to the Academic Information System
   (AIS) within **seven days** of each class.
9. A student noticing an error must raise a correction request through AIS within
   **ten working days** of the class date. Requests raised after the last day of
   instruction shall not be considered.
10. The consolidated attendance statement is displayed on AIS two weeks before the
    Major examination so that students can verify eligibility.

## AR-02.5 Relationship with leave

11. Approved medical leave (see AR-05) **does not automatically add attendance**.
    It only makes the shortfall eligible for consideration under AR-02.4.
12. There is no concept of "duty leave" that creates attendance; only Clause 4
    applies.
