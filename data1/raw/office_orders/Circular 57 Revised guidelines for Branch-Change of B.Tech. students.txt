Subject: Revised guidelines for Branch-Change of B.Tech. students
The Senate in its 40th Meeting held on 6-7th March 2025 approved revised
guidelines for Branch-Change of B.Tech. students.
The revised guidelines for the branch change criteria as given below. — -
El: The student must have completed all prescribed course credits for the previous
semesters (including the non-graded courses).
E2: The student has no backlog.
E3: No disciplinary Action has been imposed on the student.
