CIRCULAR
Subject: Discontinuation of Branch Change from Academic Year 2026-27 in UG
Programs.

The Senate, in its 43rd Meeting held on 27—28 October 2025, approved the
proposal to discontinue the provision of branch change in UG Programs at IIT Jodhpur
from the new admissions starting Academic Year 2026—27 onwards.

Accordingly, all students admitted in 2026 and thereafter shall continue in their
original allotted branches during admission at IIT Jodhpur and will not be able to change
their JoSAA-allocated branch.
